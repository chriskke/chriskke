# Hey, I'm Chris

I'll try pretty much anything at least once. Sometimes that's a new framework, sometimes it's fixing a server at 2am. Results vary.

Right now I'm an **AI Engineer** at [Calibrax AI](https://www.calibrax.ai/), a software consultancy in Malaysia.

### How I got here

I graduated from **XMUM (Xiamen University Malaysia)** in 2023 with a degree in **Digital Media Technology Engineering**. The course covered a bit of everything: game development, AR/VR, 3D modelling and animation, VFX and film, plus the software engineering to tie it together. Basically half creative, half code, and I still use both halves every day.

I didn't jump straight into coding after graduating. From 2023 to 2024 I worked with clients and small businesses, doing their marketing, shooting and editing videos, handling SEO, and building analytics systems.

What hooked me wasn't one tool or one job. It was the feeling of finding a real problem and actually fixing it. So I taught myself to build software by working on real projects. I picked up the fundamentals properly, read a lot of docs, and reached out to people who'd been there before me. Every project taught me something the last one didn't.

That's pretty much how I still learn today.

### My take on AI

Things move fast, and yes, I use AI coding agents every day. But AI is my tool, not my brain. I still want to know what it wrote and why.

Getting an app working is easy now. Keeping it up when 10,000 people hit it is the hard part. That takes real engineering, and that's what sets me apart from vibe coders.
